SEO Intelligence helps you understand how your website performs in search and where you can improve.

Use this section to review SEO health, keywords, competitors, backlinks, brand visibility, and AI visibility.

![Seo Intelligence](/logo/images/seo-intelligence.png "Seo Intelligence")

## Where To Find It

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/seo-research/domain](https://portal.wowagent.ai/seo-research/domain)

Or go to:

**SEO Intelligence**

## What You Can Do

SEO Intelligence helps you:

- Review domain SEO health
- Find keyword opportunities
- Study competitors
- Check backlinks
- Review brand mentions
- Analyze AI visibility
- Find technical SEO issues
- Plan new content topics

## SEO Performance

SEO Performance shows the SEO condition of your selected website.

Use it to understand:

- SEO score
- Search Console clicks
- Search Console impressions
- Page opportunities
- Tag Manager coverage
- Top performing pages
- Date-based SEO performance

## SEO Page Audit

SEO Page Audit helps you review page-level and technical SEO issues.

Use it to check:

- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Headings
- Canonical tags
- Indexing issues
- Broken links
- Sitemap and robots.txt
- Core web vitals or performance signals

## Domain Overview

Domain Overview gives a high-level SEO summary for your website.

Use it to understand your current position and main action items.

## Keyword Research

Keyword Research helps you discover search terms your customers may use.

Use it to find:

- Keyword ideas
- Search intent
- Content gaps
- Topic opportunities
- Possible blog topics

## Competitor Research

Competitor Research helps you compare your website with other businesses in your market.

Use it to understand:

- Who your SEO competitors are
- What topics competitors cover
- Where competitors may be stronger
- What content you should create

## Backlinks

Backlinks show websites that link to your domain.

Use this to review:

- Referring domains
- Link quality
- Anchor text
- Authority signals
- Link building opportunities

## Brand Lookup

Brand Lookup helps you review brand visibility across the web.

Use it to find:

- Brand mentions
- Reputation signals
- Citation opportunities
- Unlinked mentions

## AI Visibility

AI Visibility helps you understand how your brand may appear in AI-style search and answer engines.

Use it to review:

- AI prompt visibility
- Brand citation opportunities
- Questions users may ask AI tools
- Content gaps for AI discovery

## Recommended SEO Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with Domain Overview">
    Review your high-level SEO summary and main action items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review SEO Performance">
    Check your SEO score, Search Console data, and top performing pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run SEO Page Audit">
    Identify page-level and technical SEO issues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix technical issues first">
    Resolve the highest-impact technical problems before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Keyword Research to find topics">
    Discover keyword ideas, search intent, and content gaps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study competitors">
    Compare your website with competitors and identify content opportunities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review backlinks and brand mentions">
    Check referring domains, link quality, and brand visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use AI Visibility to improve AI discovery">
    Prepare your content for modern search behavior in AI-style engines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create content from high-value opportunities">
    Build new content based on the best opportunities you found.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

Review SEO Intelligence regularly.

Focus first on high-impact technical issues.

Create content for real customer questions.

Compare competitors but do not copy them.

Use AI Visibility to prepare your content for modern search behavior.
