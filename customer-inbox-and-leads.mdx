Customer Inbox and Leads helps you review visitor conversations and follow up with potential customers.

Use this area to understand what visitors asked, what the assistant answered, and which enquiries need action from your team.

## Customer Inbox URL

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/conversations](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/conversations)

Or go to **AI Assistant** and click **Customer Inbox**.

## Customer Enquiries URL

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/leads](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/leads)

Or, in the left menu, go to **Forms and Enquiries** and click **Customer Enquiries**. For form leads, also open **Website Forms** in the same menu and check the form's **Submissions** tab.

<Note>
  Customer Enquiries is in the **Forms and Enquiries** menu, not the **AI Assistant** menu, even though it also shows leads from the chatbot.
</Note>

## Customer Inbox

Customer Inbox shows conversations between visitors and your AI assistant. Each row is one session and shows the messages, time, page URL, rating, and whether the chat was handed off to a person.

Use it to review:

- Visitor messages in order, with timestamps
- The assistant's replies and the sources it used
- The conversation history across pages during the same visit
- Common questions asked during the week
- Missed or unclear answers, shown by a low rating or a fallback reply
- Follow-up opportunities, such as a visitor who asked about pricing but did not share a phone number
- Customer intent, such as buying, support, a demo, or a complaint
- Repeated issues, such as the same error appearing 10 times

Use these filters every day:

- **Date:** Use Today and Last 7 days for your routine check, and Last 30 days to see trends.
- **Status:** Open, Handed off, Closed, or Needs review.
- **Rating:** Look at low-rated chats first, so you can fix what the assistant knows.
- **Search:** Search by phone number, email, or a keyword like "price" or "refund".
- **Website:** Confirm that the correct website is selected, because data is saved separately for each website.

Open a chat to see the full transcript, the visitor's details, and the linked lead if one was captured. Use **Takeover** or **Reply** for high-value chats. Add an internal note for your team, such as "Called, no pickup". Internal notes are for your team only, so do not share them with the visitor.

<Warning>
  Live takeover needs a live connection. If the connection shows offline, refresh the page. When the connection shows green, new messages appear without refreshing.
</Warning>

## Why Customer Inbox Matters

Customer Inbox shows you how visitors interact with your website. It is more than a chat log: it is both product feedback and a sales queue.

You can use it to discover:

- What customers are looking for, in their own words
- Which services get the most interest, by number of requests
- Which answers need improvement, based on low ratings and repeated fallback replies
- Whether the assistant is helping properly, based on the resolution rate
- Whether visitors need human follow-up, based on handoff requests and angry language

**Weekly 15-minute routine:** Open the low-rated chats and note the top 3 bad answers. Fix them in the Knowledge Base on the same day, then test them again in the Sandbox. This loop improves conversion faster than ads.

## Customer Enquiries

Customer Enquiries shows the leads captured from visitors. Each lead is one contact to follow up with.

Leads can come from chatbot conversations, forms, or other lead capture flows, such as the pre-chat form, voice chats, and human handoffs.

Use it to review:

- The visitor's name, phone, and email
- Whether the contact details are valid, for example a 10-digit phone number in the correct format
- The visitor's message or enquiry, in their own words
- The service or product they are interested in
- A link to the full conversation for context
- The follow-up status: New, Contacted, Qualified, Won, or Lost
- Where the enquiry came from: the chatbot, a form name, a page URL, the widget, or voice
- The time, website, and workspace, so you can split leads across your team

You can filter the leads table by status, search it, and choose a time range such as 7 or 30 days. Search by phone number to avoid calling the same person twice. Open a lead to see its details, timeline, and notes.

## How To Use Conversations

Review conversations to understand what visitors need. Do not just read the latest 5 chats. Use the filters instead.

Look for:

- **Repeated questions:** If the same question appears 5 or more times, it is a missing FAQ. Add it.
- **Confusing answers:** If an answer is long, vague, or has a wrong link, shorten it and fix the link.
- **High-intent visitors:** If a visitor asked about pricing or a demo and shared their phone number, call them within 15 minutes.
- **Pricing questions:** Check that the answer matches your current price list. If it does not, update Products.
- **Support questions:** Check whether the assistant offered a handoff to a person. If it did not, fix your Bot Boundaries.
- **Product or service interest:** Each week, note the top 3 services visitors ask about, so you can focus your content and stock on them.
- **Questions the assistant could not answer:** Copy the visitor's exact wording into your FAQs.

If many visitors ask the same question, add it to the FAQs in your Knowledge Base.

Knowledge Base URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base)

To fix a question from the Inbox:

1. In the Inbox, copy the visitor's question.
2. Open the [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base) and add it as an FAQ.
3. Test the question in the Sandbox.
4. Go back to the Inbox and add a note marking it as "Fixed".

## How To Use Leads

Check new leads regularly. Check them every day during your first month, and twice a day if you have a sales team. Assign an owner to each lead so that no lead is forgotten.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Customer Enquiries">
    Filter by status **New** and date **Today**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review new leads">
    Review the new leads from top to bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read conversation context">
    Open the conversation link and read it before you call. Note the visitor's intent, budget, and city.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the customer">
    Contact the customer if they need a follow-up. Call high-intent leads, send a WhatsApp message to medium-intent leads, and email low-intent leads. Call pricing and demo leads within 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update lead status">
    Update the lead status if your panel supports it. Move the lead to **Contacted** after the call, to **Qualified** once they show interest, and to **Won** or **Lost** after the outcome. Add a note with the date and the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve Knowledge Base">
    If the same questions keep coming up, add the answers to your Knowledge Base so the assistant can handle them next time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not keep leads in **New** for more than 24 hours. Old leads left in New make it look like your team has no follow-up process.
</Warning>

## When To Follow Up Quickly

Give priority to leads when the visitor:

- Asked about pricing for a specific plan, flat size (BHK), or package
- Asked for a demo, a site visit, or a trial
- Shared their phone number or email without being asked
- Asked to speak with a person or asked for a callback
- Mentioned an urgent need, using words like "today", "tomorrow", "urgent", or "complaint"
- Asked about buying a product or service, including questions about payment or delivery
- Submitted a website form with full details
- Asked for a custom quote with a quantity or scope

**Suggested response times:**

- Urgent requests and requests to speak with a person: within 15 minutes
- Pricing and demo requests: within 2 hours
- General information requests: within 24 hours

Set up automatic alerts (in Crawlers and Jobs, or through email, push notifications, or a webhook) so your team is notified right away.

## Improve Lead Quality

To improve lead quality, improve your assistant's knowledge, your forms, and your chat flow together, instead of only chasing more leads:

- **Keep chatbot questions simple.** Ask one question at a time, and do not ask for 4 details right at the start.
- **Ask only for the contact details you need.** Name and phone are enough at first. Ask for email and city later.
- **Add clear service descriptions in Products.** Vague descriptions bring curious visitors, while specific descriptions bring buyers.
- **Add FAQs for pricing and process questions.** Hiding your prices leads to low-quality chats.
- **Use Bot Boundaries to set rules for human handoff.** For example, hand off to a person when a visitor demands a refund, is angry, has a high-value enquiry above 50k, or asks for medical or legal advice.
- **Test your forms after you create or edit them.** Submit a test entry and check that it appears in both Submissions and Leads.
- **Check the pre-chat form setting in [Chatbot Management](/chatbot-management).** Turning it ON gives you fewer leads, but they are more serious. Turning it OFF gives you more chats, but they are less serious. Use ON for high-value services.
- **Review every week** which pages bring junk leads, then fix the content on those pages and the chatbot greeting for those URLs.

## If Leads Are Not Appearing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Widget is installed correctly">
    Open your live website in an incognito window. Check that the chat bubble is visible and that it opens and closes. If it is missing, go to the [Integration Center](/integration-center) and click **Verify**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Website form is active">
    Open **Forms** and check that the form's status in the listing is **Active**. Also check that the form is embedded on your live page with the correct formKey.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Integration setup is complete">
    The Integration Center must show green for the widget and all required items. If an item is red, your live website traffic is not reaching WOWagent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Correct website is selected">
    The website switcher must match the domain where the visitor chatted, because data is saved separately for each website.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Visitor submitted the form successfully">
    Check for validation errors, such as a phone number that is not 10 digits or an empty required field. Submit a test entry yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You are checking the correct workspace">
    The workspace switcher at the top left must match the workspace you were invited to. The wrong workspace shows zero leads.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You have permission to view leads">
    Viewer or Custom roles can hide phone numbers or the whole Leads tab. Ask your Admin to check your permissions in User Management.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connection and filters">
    Clear the search, status, and date range filters. Many users filter by Today, but the lead arrived yesterday.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credits and jobs">
    If the widget or the website crawl is blocked, no conversations are created, so no leads appear either. Check the last crawl time in [Website Overview](/website-overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If the issue continues, create a support ticket and include:

- Your website domain
- The date range you checked
- The phone number of your test lead and the time you submitted it
- Screenshots of your Inbox filters, your Integration status, and your Forms listing

Do not include full OTPs or passwords.
