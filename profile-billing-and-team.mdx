Profile Billing And Team helps you manage your account, subscription, credits, workspace users, and team access.

Use this section when you need to update account details, manage billing, invite users, or review account activity.

![Billing New](/logo/images/billing-new.png "Billing New")

## Billing URL

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing](https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing)

Or go to:

**Accounts > Billing**

## Profile

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/profile](https://portal.wowagent.ai/profile)

Or go to:

**Accounts > Profile**

The **Account Profile** page lets you manage your personal identity, company details, and tax receipt information. The header card shows your name, account status, email, company, mobile number, and whether a GSTIN is linked.

Use the switcher at the top right (or the cards on the left) to move between two sections:

- **Basic Profile**: personal and company identity
- **Billing & GST**: GSTIN and tax receipt address

### Basic Profile

![Basic Profile](/images/profile-basic.png "Basic Profile")

The **Personal & Account Information** section shows your contact details. Click **Edit** (or **Edit Profile**) to update them.

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Full Name | Required. Your name as shown across the account. |
| Email Address | Locked. Your login email cannot be changed here. |
| Company Name | Your company or business name. |
| Mobile Number | Your contact number with country code. |

To change your profile image, click the pencil icon on your avatar.

### Billing & GST

![Billing and GST](/images/gst.png "Billing and GST")

The **GST & Receipt Details** section holds optional tax information that is applied to your subscription and top-up receipts. Click **Edit** (or **Edit Billing Details**) to update it.

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Billing / Entity Name | The name to print on your receipts. |
| GSTIN (GST Number) | Your 15-digit GST number. Optional. |
| Billing Street Address | Street address for your receipts. |
| City | Billing city. |
| State | Billing state. |
| PIN / Postal Code | Billing PIN or postal code. |
| Country | Billing country. |

<Info>
  18% GST applies to all plans and top-ups in India. Link your valid 15-digit GSTIN under Billing & GST so your official tax receipts reflect your company registration for GST input tax credit.
</Info>

Keep your profile and billing details updated so your team, support records, and tax receipts stay accurate.

## Billing

Use Billing to review and manage:

- Current plan
- Subscription status
- Credits
- Credit top-up (packs of 100, 500, or 1000 credits, or a custom amount)
- Upgrade options
- Downgrade options
- Transaction history
- Payment records

## Subscription Plans

Billing shows the available subscription plans.

Review the plan details carefully before switching.

Each plan can have different limits for:

- Number of websites
- User seats
- AI credits
- Chatbots
- Voice or avatar features
- Team controls
- Content or SEO tools

For prices, plan features, and a full comparison, see [Plans and Credits](/plans-credits-and-limits).

## Credits

Some WOWagent actions use credits.

Examples include AI tasks, scans, content generation, and other usage-based features.

If you do not have enough credits, the panel shows an insufficient credit message.

To continue, review your plan or add credits from Billing. See [Buy Additional Credits](/plans-credits-and-limits#buy-additional-credits).

## User Management

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/user-management](https://portal.wowagent.ai/user-management)

Use User Management to invite team members and manage access.

This is useful when multiple people need to work on the same website or workspace.

## Workspaces

WOWagent supports workspace access.

A workspace helps separate account access, websites, permissions, and team activity.

If you are invited to a workspace, accept the invitation and select the correct workspace from the panel.

## Activity Log

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/activity-log](https://portal.wowagent.ai/activity-log)

Activity Log helps you review important actions in the account.

Use it to check what changed and when.

## Support Tickets

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/support-tickets](https://portal.wowagent.ai/support-tickets)

Use Support Tickets when you need help from the WOWagent team.

Create a ticket for:

- Setup problems
- Integration issues
- Billing questions
- Website scan problems
- Chatbot issues
- Lead capture issues
- Account access problems

To get a faster answer, include these details in your ticket:

- Your website domain and workspace name
- The page where the issue happened
- What you clicked or tried
- What you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot of the issue or error message

<Tip>
  Before you create a ticket, check [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/faq). Many common issues have a quick fix there.
</Tip>

## Website Deactivation

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/danger-zone](https://portal.wowagent.ai/danger-zone)

Use this only when you want to deactivate or remove a website.

<Warning>
  Be careful. Deactivation can affect tracking, chatbot availability, reports, and website workflows.
</Warning>

## Recommended Account Routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keep profile details updated">
    Make sure your name, email, and contact details are correct so you receive account and billing messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review billing and credits regularly">
    Check your Credit Balance and cycle end date in Billing so you do not run out of credits during important work. See [Plans and Credits](/plans-credits-and-limits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite only trusted team members">
    Give access only to people who need it, and remove users who no longer work on your websites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Activity Log for important changes">
    Review the Activity Log to see who changed settings, content, or access in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Support Tickets for issues that need help">
    If you cannot fix an issue yourself, create a support ticket with your domain, workspace, the page, and a screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Be careful before deactivating a website">
    Deactivating a website stops its WOWagent features. Confirm it is the correct website before you continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>
