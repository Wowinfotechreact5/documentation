Use this page if you cannot log in because you forgot your password. You can reset your password only through an email link. WOWagent does not use OTP or SMS for password resets.

In short: request a reset link from the Login page, open the email, set a new password, and log in again.

![Forgot](/logo/images/forgotpass.png "Forgot")

## Step 1: Request Reset Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Login page">
    Open [https://portal.wowagent.ai/login](https://portal.wowagent.ai/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot Password">
    Click **Forgot Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your registered email">
    Enter your registered email address without any spaces. It must be the same email you used when you signed up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Email Sent popup">
    An **Email Sent** popup appears with the message "We have sent link to your email".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Login">
    Click **Go to Login** and wait for the email to arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  WOWagent always shows the success message, even if the email is not found. This stops anyone from guessing which accounts exist. Check the spelling of your email carefully, because you will not receive any email if the address is wrong.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The reset link is valid for 1 hour only, so request it once. If you submit several requests, use only the latest email, because older links stop working.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Open Email Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the WOWagent email">
    Look for an email from **WOWagent** with the subject **Reset Your Password - WOWagent**. Check your Inbox, Promotions, Spam, and Junk folders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Reset">
    Click the **Reset** button or link. Each link is unique to your reset request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset Password page opens">
    The **Reset Password** page opens with the **New Password** and **Confirm Password** fields.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the email has not arrived, wait up to 5 minutes, then:

- Check your Spam and Junk folders, and add WOWagent to your safe sender list.
- Check that you spelled your email correctly in Step 1.
- Make sure your mailbox is not full.
- If it still has not arrived, go back to the Login page and request a new link with **Forgot Password**.

## Step 3: Set New Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter New Password">
    Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Confirm Password">
    Type the same password again. It must match the New Password exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the success popup">
    A **Password Reset Successfully** popup appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Go to Login">
    Click **Go to Login**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in with your email and your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The reset form follows these rules:

- All fields are required.
- Both passwords must match. Otherwise, you see the error "Passwords do not match".
- The password must meet the password policy. Otherwise, you see a policy error.
- The reset link must be valid. Otherwise, you see the error "Invalid reset token".
- If more than 1 hour has passed, you see the error "Reset link expired" and need to request a new link.

<Tip>
  Type your password manually instead of copying and pasting it. Copying often adds a space at the start or end, which makes the reset fail.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No email received">
    The email address may be wrong, the email may be in your spam folder, or delivery may be delayed. Wait up to 5 minutes, check Spam and Junk, then request a new link from the Login page if needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invalid reset token">
    The link is broken, you clicked an old link, or the link was already used. Request a new link and use the latest email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reset link expired">
    More than 1 hour has passed since you requested the link. Request a new link and complete the reset within 1 hour.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Passwords do not match">
    Type both passwords again. Check that Caps Lock is off, and use the show password button to confirm what you typed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Password policy fail">
    Add whatever is missing (an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, or a special character) and make sure the password has at least 8 characters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still logged in to an old session after reset">
    Log out from all tabs, clear your browser cache, and log in again with your new password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account locked or 403 verify email">
    Verify your email first from the Login popup, then reset your password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still stuck">
    Open [Support Tickets](https://portal.wowagent.ai/support-tickets) in the portal and create a ticket with your registered email, the time of your reset request, and a screenshot. Never include your password in the ticket.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
