Login is how you enter the WOWagent panel. Without login, Dashboard, Chatbot, Leads, and all other pages stay locked.

![Login](/logo/images/Login.png "Login")

## Where To Find It

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/login](https://portal.wowagent.ai/login)

After login, you go to the Dashboard automatically. If it is your first time, you go to Onboard to add a website.

## How To Login With Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Email Address">
    No spaces. Example: `you@example.com`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Password">
    Use the show and hide button to check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Log In">
    Click **Log In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait 2 to 3 seconds">
    Do not double-click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Rules

- Email must be in a valid format with `@` and a domain.
- Email cannot contain spaces. Spaces are automatically removed.
- Password cannot contain spaces.
- Password is case sensitive. Check Caps Lock if needed.

## How To Login With Google

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Continue with Google">
    Click **Continue with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Google email">
    Select the same Google email every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow the popup">
    If the popup is blocked, allow popups for the portal site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms (first time only)">
    If it is your first time, accept the Workspace Terms and Privacy Policy when asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Dashboard">
    You will go to the Dashboard automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you change your Google email, you may get an error like `GOOGLE EMAIL CHANGED`. Always use the original email.

  If you get an error like `GOOGLE ACCOUNT CONFLICT`, use email login instead and contact support.
</Warning>

## Email Not Verified Case

If you see the message **Please verify your email**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Verify Your Email popup">
    A popup shows **Verify Your Email** with a **Resend** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Resend">
    Click **Resend**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email folders">
    Check your Inbox, Promotions, Spam, and Junk folders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the verification link">
    Open the email and click the verification link within 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in again">
    Come back to Login and log in again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The verification link is valid for 24 hours only. After it expires, click **Resend** again.
</Note>

After successful verification, you return to the Login page and can log in.

If verification fails because the link is invalid, the link has expired, or a server error occurs, request a new verification link.

## Invite Login Case

If a team member invites you with an invitation link:

- If you already have an account, log in first with the invited email, then click **Accept** and join the workspace.
- If you get `INVITATION EMAIL MISMATCH`, log out and log in with the invited email address instead of another email.
- After accepting the invitation, the workspace is selected and you will be taken to the page allowed by your role.

If the invitation shows **unavailable**, **expired**, or **declined**, ask the workspace owner to send a new invitation.

## Forgot Password

On the Login page, click **Forgot Password** and follow the email reset link. The link is valid for 1 hour.

<Note>
  OTP screens in the UI are not used. Only the email reset link is valid. If the link says `Invalid reset token` or `Reset link expired`, request a new reset link.
</Note>

For the full steps and password rules, see [Forgot Password](/forgot-password).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Invalid email or password">
    Check the email, password, spaces, and Caps Lock. Try Forgot Password if you are not sure about the password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Session expired or 401">
    Your login session has ended. Log in again. Do not use the browser back button after logout.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verify modal loop">
    Check your Spam/Junk folder, add the sender to your safe list, and resend the verification email after 1 minute.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Google button loading for a long time or not loading">
    Check your internet connection, allow Google Accounts scripts, disable ad blockers, and reload the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="PLAN SEAT LIMIT EXCEEDED">
    If this appears during Google login with an invitation, the workspace has reached its member limit. Ask the workspace owner to upgrade the plan or remove a member.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="LEGAL ACCEPTANCE REQUIRED">
    Accept the Terms checkbox and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still stuck">
    Create a Support Ticket with your email, a screenshot of the issue, and the time when the issue occurred.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
