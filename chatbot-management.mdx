Chatbot Management is where you manage the AI assistant that appears on your website.

Use this page to configure how your assistant looks, behaves, speaks, and helps visitors.

![Chatbot Management](/logo/images/chatbot-management.png "Chatbot Management")

## Where To Find It

Open:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent)

Or go to **AI Assistant** and click **Chatbot Management**.

First, select the correct website from the website switcher. Chatbot settings are saved separately for each website, so when you switch websites, the name, greeting, color, and all other settings change too. If no website is selected, the page is empty.

## What You Can Manage

In Chatbot Management, you can manage:

- **Assistant name:** Shown in the widget header.
- **Assistant behavior:** Tone, language, auto start, and handoff to a person.
- **Chat widget settings:** Position, style, color, and layout.
- **Widget appearance:** Bubble or full window, movement, branding, and background.
- **Avatar or voice options (if enabled):** A 3D avatar and a voice.
- **Deployment status:** Draft, tested in Sandbox, or live and verified.
- **Visitor interaction settings:** Pre-chat form, feedback form, and info link buttons.
- **Lead capture behavior:** When to ask for the visitor's name, phone, and email.

Click **Save** after every change. Some changes also need you to click **Publish** and verify the widget again before they show on your live website. Test in the Sandbox first, then check your live website in an incognito window.

## Main Configuration Areas

### Bot Configuration

Use Bot Configuration to set up the main chatbot behavior.

This includes:

- **Public widget status:** ON lets your live website show the widget. OFF hides it everywhere, even if the script is installed.
- **Bot name:** For example, "WOW Helper". Keep it short and in line with your brand. The name appears in the widget header, the inbox, and chat transcripts.
- **Welcome greeting:** The first message visitors see, for example "Hi, how can I help you today?". Keep it under 120 characters and include one question to start the chat.
- **Conversational tone:** Friendly, Professional, or Playful. Friendly suits retail, while Professional suits B2B businesses, legal firms, and clinics. The tone must match your brand voice.
- **Language selection:** The default chat language and other supported languages. If your visitors mix Hindi and English, check how the bot handles it.
- **Speed control:** The typing delay and reply speed. Slower replies feel more human, while faster replies feel more like a bot. Keep it at medium for sales.

<Tip>
  Change one field at a time, click Save, and test it in the Sandbox before moving to the next one. Many changes at once are hard to troubleshoot.
</Tip>

### Widget Layout

Use Widget Layout to choose how the chatbot appears on your website.

The options include:

- **Full chatbot:** A full chat window with history, avatar, and quick replies. Best for desktop and service websites, but it takes up more space.
- **Bar only:** A small bar at the bottom that expands when clicked. Best for mobile and minimal websites, because it is less intrusive.
- **3D avatar:** An avatar with a face, voice, and video-style experience. Best for premium and demo websites. It needs more bandwidth, so test it on mobile data.

**How to choose:** Use **Full chatbot** for service and lead generation websites, **Bar only** for ecommerce and blogs, and **3D avatar** for showcase and high-value businesses. You can change the layout later without losing your Knowledge Base. After changing the layout, check the mobile view.

### Style

Use Style settings to control how the widget looks.

You can configure:

- **Primary widget color:** Your brand color as a hex code. For example, blue builds trust and green suits health. Check that white text is easy to read on it.
- **Widget position:** Bottom right is standard. Use bottom left if another bubble is already on the right. Make sure the widget does not cover your Add to Cart or Call button.
- **Widget movement:** Bounce, slide, or static. Subtle movement gets clicks, while heavy movement annoys visitors, so keep it subtle.
- **Branding badge:** Show or hide the "Powered by" line. Paid plans can hide it, depending on the plan rules.
- **Background effect:** Solid, blur, or transparent. Blur looks premium, but test that text is still easy to read.
- **3D avatar behavior:** Idle motion, waving when opened, and lip sync with the voice. Turn off motion if your website is slow.

Always preview the widget on both desktop and mobile after a style change. A color that looks good on desktop can be hard to see on a phone in sunlight.

### Behaviour

Use Behaviour settings to control how the assistant acts during visitor sessions.

You can configure:

- **Auto start session:** When ON, the chat opens automatically after a few seconds. This works well for offers but can annoy visitors, so use a delay instead of opening instantly. When OFF, the chat waits for the visitor to click, which is the safer option.
- **Page transition loader:** Shows a loader when the assistant takes visitors to another page through AI Navigation. Keep it ON for a smooth experience.
- **Pre-chat visitor form:** Asks for the visitor's name, phone, and email before the chat starts. ON gives you more leads, but fewer chats start. OFF gives you more chats, but less contact information. Use ON for high-value services and OFF for support.
- **Feedback widget form:** Shows thumbs up, thumbs down, and a comment box after answers. Keep it ON for the first month to find bad answers, then decide whether to keep it.
- **Info link button:** Adds buttons such as Pricing, Contact, and Book Demo under answers. Link them to the correct pages and check the links every month.

Behaviour settings directly affect how many leads you get. If your leads are low, check how your pre-chat form, greeting, and auto start settings work together.

### Avatar

If available, choose a 3D avatar for voice and video interactions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Avatar tab">
    Preview 3 to 4 avatars with their voice samples.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that the voice and language match">
    For example, an English female voice may need a separate test if your visitors mix Hindi and English.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the avatar and voice, then Save">
    Test in the Sandbox with voice ON, and check the lip sync and speed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check load time on mobile data">
    If it loads slowly, use a static avatar or the Bar only layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The avatar changes only how answers are presented, not the answers themselves. Answers come from your Knowledge Base and bot settings. If the voice mispronounces your brand name, change its spelling in the pronunciation field of your Business Profile, or choose a different voice.

## Before You Configure Chatbot

Before changing chatbot settings, complete your [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base) first.

Your assistant gives better answers when it has accurate business information.

At minimum, add:

- **Business profile:** Name, about, location, contact details, website, and social links.
- **Business hours:** Opening and closing times for each day, your timezone, and holidays.
- **Products and services:** At least 1 to 2, each with a name, price, and link.
- **FAQs:** At least 5 real questions and answers about pricing, contact, delivery, refunds, and timings.
- **Website pages:** At least 3 URLs to crawl, such as your home, pricing, and contact pages.
- **Uploaded documents:** Policies, brochures, or menu PDFs. Wait until processing is done.
- **Bot boundaries:** What the bot must NOT do, along with tone and handoff rules.

**Why this order matters:** Settings without knowledge give polite but wrong answers. Knowledge without settings gives correct answers in a badly styled widget. Add your knowledge first, then configure the chatbot, and then test.

## Widget Setup

The chatbot appears on your website through a widget.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integration Center">
    Follow the widget installation instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the widget script to your website's head section on every page">
    Verify the widget from WOWagent. The status must turn green.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your website and confirm the assistant appears">
    Check in an incognito window and on mobile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Copy the script (it includes your `data-key`) and add it site-wide so every page loads it. For WordPress, use a Header and Footer plugin. For Google Tag Manager, use a Custom HTML tag with the All Pages trigger, then publish. Clear your website cache and CDN cache after adding the script, and turn off your ad blocker while testing. After a theme change, verify again, because theme changes often remove the script. For full steps, see [Install Widget](/install-widget).

<Note>
  The Sandbox chat works even if the live widget is not verified, but your live website needs a verified widget. The Publish button stays disabled until verification turns green.
</Note>

## Test The Assistant

After setup, test the assistant like a real visitor. Use the Sandbox first, then your live website.

Ask questions such as:

- What services do you offer?
- What are your prices?
- Where are you located?
- What are your working hours?
- How can I contact support?
- Can I book a demo?
- Can I speak to a human?

For each answer, check that:

- The facts, links, and phone number are correct.
- The tone matches your setting.
- The language is correct.
- Handoff to a person happens when it should. For example, "Can I speak to a human?" must start a handoff, not repeat the same reply.
- The lead form appears when you set it to.
- The assistant does not make anything up, such as a fake discount or a fake address.

Test at least 10 questions, and test on mobile too. Include a typo, such as "pricee", to check how well the assistant handles mistakes.

## Improve Bad Answers

If the assistant gives a wrong or incomplete answer, update the Knowledge Base. Changing the tone alone does not fix it.

Usually, you need to improve:

- **FAQs:** Add the exact question wording visitors use, with a short and clear answer.
- **Products and services:** Fix the price, description, or link.
- **Business profile:** Fix the address, phone, email, or hours.
- **Uploaded documents:** Replace an old PDF with the new version and wait for it to be processed again.
- **Bot boundaries:** Add a rule such as "Do not guess prices. Ask visitors to check the pricing page, and hand off to a person."

Then test the same question again in the Sandbox. If it is fixed, test 2 related questions to make sure nothing else changed. If it is still wrong, check that the correct page was crawled in **Website Pages**, and check **Crawler Configuration** to see whether the page is blocked. To keep your Knowledge Base fresh, turn on automatic Knowledge Base sync in **Crawlers & Jobs**.

## Recommended Checklist

Before going live, confirm that:

- The widget is installed on every page and verified green.
- The widget is visible on your website in an incognito window and on mobile, and it opens and closes cleanly.
- The assistant answers common questions correctly, with the right links.
- Contact details are correct in both answers and buttons.
- Business hours are correct, including holidays.
- Lead capture works, and a test lead appears in Leads.
- Conversations appear in Customer Inbox with their ratings.
- Enquiries appear in Customer Enquiries, and in Forms submissions if you use a form.

If any item fails, fix it, click **Save**, and test that item and one related item again. Do not start running ads until every item is green. During the first week, check the Inbox every day for bad answers and fix them in the Knowledge Base on the same day.
