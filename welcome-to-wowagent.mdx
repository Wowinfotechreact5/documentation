WOWagent helps you manage your website, AI assistant, leads, SEO, security, content, forms, and team access from one panel.

Use this guide if you are using WOWagent for the first time or want to understand how each part of the panel works.

![Dashboard](/logo/images/dashboard.png "Dashboard")

## What You Can Do With WOWagent

### Website Management

Connect your website, view website health, monitor performance, check SSL, review DNS, and run website audits.

Panel URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/dashboard](https://portal.wowagent.ai/dashboard)

### AI Assistant

Create and manage your AI assistant for website visitors.

You can configure chatbot behavior, widget style, voice/avatar options, and visitor interaction settings.

Chatbot URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent)

### Knowledge Base

Train your AI assistant with business details, services, FAQs, website pages, uploaded documents, working hours, and rules.

Knowledge Base URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base)

### Customer Inbox

View conversations between visitors and your AI assistant.

Customer Inbox URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/conversations](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/conversations)

### Leads And Enquiries

Capture customer enquiries through the chatbot and website forms.

Customer Enquiries URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/leads](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/leads)

Forms URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/forms](https://portal.wowagent.ai/forms)

### SEO Intelligence

Review SEO health, keyword opportunities, competitors, backlinks, brand visibility, and AI visibility.

SEO Intelligence URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/seo-research/domain](https://portal.wowagent.ai/seo-research/domain)

### AI Growth

Generate content, manage your content calendar, and review published articles.

AI Content Generator URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-content-generator](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-content-generator)

### Account Management

Manage your profile, billing, subscription, credits, team members, support tickets, and activity logs.

Billing URL:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing](https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing)

## Recommended Setup Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or log in to your account">
    New to WOWagent? [Sign up](/signup) for an account. If you already have one, [log in](/login) to the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    [Add your website](/add-your-website) so WOWagent can link your assistant, leads, SEO, and health checks to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Integration Center setup">
    In the [Integration Center](/integration-center), verify the chat widget and connect the services you need, such as Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train your AI assistant using Knowledge Base">
    Add your website pages, documents, and FAQs to the [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base) so the assistant gives correct answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your chatbot">
    In [Chatbot Management](/chatbot-management), set your assistant's name, greeting, tone, and appearance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the assistant on your website">
    Open your live website, ask the assistant a few real customer questions, and check its answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review leads, conversations, SEO, and security reports">
    Check [leads and conversations](/customer-inbox-and-leads), [SEO](/seo-intelligence), and [website health](/website-health-and-security) regularly to follow up and improve.
  </Step>
</Steps>
