Signing up creates your account, your workspace, and your free trial in one step. You only need to sign up once. After that, use [Login](/login) to access WOWagent.

![Signup](/logo/images/Signups.png "Signup")

## Where To Find It

Open the signup page:

[https://portal.wowagent.ai/sign-up](https://portal.wowagent.ai/sign-up)

After you sign up, a **Verify Your Email** popup appears. From there, go to the Login page.

## How To Sign Up

Fill in all the fields, then accept the Terms:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Full Name">
    Use letters and spaces only. Do not use numbers or start with a space. Example: Rahul Sharma
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email Address">
    Enter a valid email address without spaces. Example: `you@example.com`. WOWagent saves your email in lowercase automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mobile Number">
    Enter digits only, up to 10 digits, starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9 (Indian format). Example: 9876543210
  </Step>
  <Step title="Password">
    Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. A live checklist turns green as you meet each rule. Do not start the password with a space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Referral Code (optional)">
    If you opened a referral link, the code is filled in for you. You can also type it manually. WOWagent checks the code, and if it is invalid, a **Referral Code Not Applied** popup appears. You can then choose **Continue Without Referral** or **Fix Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept Terms">
    Check the box to accept the Workspace Terms and Privacy Policy (version 2026-08-13). This step is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Account">
    Click **Create Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Login">
    A **Verify Your Email** popup appears and asks you to check your Inbox and Spam folders. Click **Go to Login**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Verify your email within 24 hours, then log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You must accept the Terms and Privacy Policy to create an account. If you do not check the box, you see the error "LEGAL ACCEPTANCE REQUIRED".
</Warning>

## Password Rules In Detail

Your password must meet all of these rules:

- It is between 8 and 128 characters long.
- It has at least one uppercase letter (A to Z).
- It has at least one lowercase letter (a to z).
- It has at least one number (0 to 9).
- It has at least one special character, such as @, #, \$, or \*.
- It does not start with a space.

When all the rules turn green, the checklist hides automatically after 4 seconds.

<Tip>
  For a password that is strong and easy to remember, use 12 or more characters that mix words, numbers, and a symbol.
</Tip>

## Referral Code In Detail

- If you opened a referral link, a banner shows that the code is applied.
- If you type the code manually, WOWagent checks it automatically a moment after you stop typing.
- An invalid code does not block your signup. You can continue without it.
- A valid code links your signup to the person who referred you, so they can receive rewards.

To fix a code, type it again, wait for the green tick, and then submit the form.

## What Happens Next

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account created">
    Your account is created, but it is not verified yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification email sent">
    WOWagent sends you an email with a verification link that is valid for 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the link">
    WOWagent verifies your email and starts your free trial if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Login">
    You return to the Login page with your email verified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    When you log in for the first time, your trial is confirmed and your workspace is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    Go to the Onboard page to add your website. See [Add Your Website](/add-your-website).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you see "Email already registered", go to [Login](/login) instead. If you forgot your password, use [Forgot Password](/forgot-password).
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Name error">
    Remove any numbers or symbols, and remove any space at the start of the name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email error">
    Remove any spaces and check that the email has an @ and a dot in the right places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Mobile error">
    Enter exactly 10 digits starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9. Do not add +91 or spaces.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Password stays red">
    Make sure your password meets all 5 rules. Copying and pasting can add a hidden space, so type it manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Must accept Terms">
    Tick the checkbox (scroll through the terms if needed), then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No email">
    Check your Spam, Junk, and Promotions folders. Click **Resend**, wait 2 minutes, and check that you spelled your email correctly during signup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Link expired">
    Send a new verification email from the popup on the Login page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Google signup conflict like EMAIL VERIFICATION REQUIRED">
    Verify your email first, then log in with Google.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still stuck">
    Create a support ticket with your name, your email, and a screenshot of the problem.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
