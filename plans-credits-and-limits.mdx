WOWagent offers three subscription plans: **Launch**, **Growth**, and **Scale**. Each plan includes a set number of websites, user seats, and monthly AI credits. You can pay monthly or annually, and you can buy extra credits at any time from [Billing](https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing).

<Info>
  All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹). 18% GST is added on top of the base price for every plan and credit purchase.
</Info>

## Monthly or Annual Billing

At the top of the Subscription Plans section, choose **Monthly** or **Annually**. You can switch between them anytime.

<Tip>
  Annual billing includes **Bonus Credits and Days**. Select **Annually** in Billing to see the annual price and bonus for each plan.
</Tip>

## Upgrade or Downgrade Your Plan

Open [Billing](https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing), review the plans, and click the button on the plan you want. The button shows the type of change, for example **Buy Scale** or **Downgrade to Growth**.

<Warning>
  If you downgrade to a plan that allows fewer websites than you currently have active, Billing shows a message such as "Deactivate 1 website(s) before switching to this plan." Deactivate the extra websites first, then switch plans. See [Profile and Billing](/profile-billing-and-team).
</Warning>

When you select a lower plan, Billing also shows "This is a downgrade from your current plan." Check the websites, seats, and credits in the new plan before you confirm.

## How AI Credits Work

AI credits are used for billable actions in WOWagent, such as AI tasks, website scans, and content generation. The **Credit Balance** card in Billing shows:

- **Available balance:** the total credits you can use for billable actions.
- **Monthly plan:** the credits that come from your current plan.
- **Cycle ends:** the date your current billing cycle ends.

To see how each action uses credits, click **Know more** on the Credit Balance card.

If you do not have enough credits, the panel shows an insufficient credit message. Buy more credits or upgrade your plan to continue.

## Buy Additional Credits

You can buy extra credits at any time. Payment is processed securely through **PhonePe**, and purchased credits are added to your balance after payment is confirmed.

### Custom Credits

If you need a different amount, buy custom credits:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to [Billing](https://portal.wowagent.ai/billing) and find **Add Required Infra Credits** under Buy Additional Credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the number of credits">
    Type the number of credits you need in the **Enter Custom Credit** field. Use whole numbers only, from a minimum of 100 credits up to less than 1,000,000 credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay securely">
    Click **Buy custom credits** and complete the payment through PhonePe. The price is ₹1 per credit plus 18% GST. For example, 2,500 credits cost ₹2,500 + ₹450 GST = ₹2,950.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balance">
    After the payment is confirmed, the credits appear in your Credit Balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  GST applies to every purchase, even if you do not have a GSTIN. Adding billing details is optional.
</Note>

## Transaction and Receipt History

The **Transaction and Receipt History** section in Billing lists your payments. Choose a date range to filter the list. For each transaction, you can see:

- Receipt and order ID
- Plan and price
- Provider ID
- Created date
- Status

Use the **Action** column to download receipts for paid transactions.
