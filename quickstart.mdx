Quickstart helps you go from your first login to a working WOWagent setup.

Use this page when you want the fastest setup path. Follow Steps 1 to 7 in order, because each step unlocks the next one. A basic setup takes about 15 to 20 minutes in total.

![Onboarding Flow](/logo/images/onboarding-flow.png "Onboarding Flow")

## Step 1: Log In

Open the WOWagent panel and log in.

Login URL: [https://portal.wowagent.ai/login](https://portal.wowagent.ai/login)

**New user:** Create your account on the Sign Up page, verify your email within 24 hours, and then log in with your email and password. For full steps and field rules, see [Signup](/signup).

**Google login:** Click **Continue with Google** and use the same email every time. If it is your first time, accept the Terms when asked.

**Team invite:** If your team invited you, open the invitation link from your email:

- **New to WOWagent:** Enter your name and password, accept the Terms, and click **Accept invitation**.
- **Already have an account:** Log in first, then accept the invitation and join the workspace.

**If login fails:**

- `Invalid email or password`: Check for extra spaces, capital letters, and that you are using the correct email.
- `Please verify email`: Click **Resend**, check your inbox, verify your email, and then log in.
- `Session expired`: Log in again.
- **Forgot your password?** Reset it with the link sent to your email. The link is valid for 1 hour. See [Forgot Password](/forgot-password).

For more login help, see [Login](/login).

After you log in, you land on the Dashboard. If it is your first time, WOWagent takes you to `/onboard` automatically so you can add your website.

## Step 2: Add Your Website

Open onboarding: [https://portal.wowagent.ai/onboard](https://portal.wowagent.ai/onboard)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Get Started">
    Select the correct workspace if asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Business Name">
    Use the official name your customers know, for example **WOWagent**. This field is required and allows up to 40 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a clean domain">
    Example: **example.com**

    Do not enter a full URL such as `https://example.com` or a page address such as `www.example.com/contact`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Analyze Website">
    Wait 5 to 15 seconds and do not double-click. When it succeeds, you see **Website Added Successfully** and go to the main panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Type only the root domain. Do not include `https://`, `http://`, `www.`, a page path such as `/contact`, a `?query`, spaces, or a port. WOWagent changes the domain to lowercase and removes `www.` automatically.
</Warning>

**If it fails:**

- `invalid domain`: Type the domain again in the format `example.com`.
- `already registered by you`: Select the website from the website switcher instead of adding it again.
- `PLAN_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`: Your plan has reached its website limit. Go to Billing to upgrade. Only the workspace owner can do this.
- `site not live`: Open your domain in an incognito window and fix any DNS, hosting, or SSL issues first. A website running only on your own computer (localhost) will fail.

For full details, see [Add Your Website](/add-your-website).

## Step 3: Complete Integration Center

Open Integration Center: [https://portal.wowagent.ai/website/integrations](https://portal.wowagent.ai/website/integrations)

<Note>
  Select your website from the website switcher first. Otherwise, the page is empty.
</Note>

Complete the required setup items, such as:

- **Widget verification:** Copy the widget script (`<script src=...widget.js data-key=...>`) and paste it before the closing `</head>` tag on your website, in your WordPress header and footer settings, or in a GTM Custom HTML tag. Then click **Verify**. The status must turn green, because live chat never appears without this step. For full steps, see [Install Widget](/install-widget).
- **Pixel setup:** If shown, copy the pixel code and add it to your website's head section. It is used for tracking.
- **Google Analytics:** Click **Connect**, allow access with your Google account, select your property, and click **Save**. You need this for traffic insights.
- **Tag Manager:** Connect your container if you use GTM to add the widget.
- **Search Console:** Connect it to get keyword data and submit your sitemap.
- **Blog or publishing setup:** Only needed if you use AI blogs. Set up the Cloudflare path, add the DNS CNAME record, and click **Verify**.
- **DNS or Cloudflare setup (if required):** Add the CNAME or TXT record shown to your domain's DNS (for example, at GoDaddy, Cloudflare, or Hostinger). Wait 5 to 30 minutes, then click **Verify**.

<Warning>
  Do not skip the **Verify** clicks. Each card must show Connected or Verified. After a website redesign, verify again, because redesigns often remove the script.
</Warning>

## Step 4: Train Your Assistant

Open Knowledge Base: [https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base)

For good answers, add at least the following:

- **Business profile:** Name, about, location, contact details, website, and social links. The assistant uses this in answers and reports.
- **Business hours:** Opening and closing times for each day, your timezone, and holidays. The assistant uses this to answer questions about when you are available.
- **Products and services:** At least 1 to 2 items, each with a name, description, price, and link. You need these for product questions.
- **FAQs:** At least 5 real customer questions and answers, covering pricing, contact, delivery, refunds, and timings.
- **Website pages:** 3 to 5 important URLs to crawl, such as your home, pricing, contact, and services pages. WOWagent reads these pages to train your assistant.
- **Documents:** Upload PDF or DOCX files such as policies, brochures, or menus. Wait until processing is complete.
- **Bot boundaries:** Set what the assistant must NOT do (for example, no fake discounts, no legal advice, and hand off to a person when unsure), along with tone and handoff rules.

<Tip>
  Keep automatic Knowledge Base sync ON in **Crawlers & Jobs** so your assistant stays up to date after website changes.
</Tip>

## Step 5: Configure Chatbot

Open Chatbot Management: [https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent](https://portal.wowagent.ai/ai-agent)

Set up:

- **Bot name:** For example, "WOW Helper". It is shown in the widget header.
- **Welcome greeting:** The first message visitors see. For example, "Hi! How can I help you today?"
- **Conversational tone:** Friendly, Professional, or Playful. It must match your brand.
- **Language selection:** The default chat language and other supported languages.
- **Widget layout:** Position (bottom right or bottom left), color, and style (bubble or full window).
- **Widget style:** Primary color, avatar shape, and header text.
- **Behavior settings:** Turn the pre-chat form ON or OFF (it collects the visitor's name and phone number before the chat), and set auto start and the feedback form.
- **Avatar options:** If you use voice, choose an avatar and a voice. Preview them before saving.

<Tip>
  Click **Save**, then test in the Sandbox chat on the same page before going live. The Sandbox works even if the live widget is not verified yet, so it is good for a quick check.
</Tip>

## Step 6: Test Everything

Before going live, test in 2 places: the Sandbox and your live website.

Test checklist:

- **Common customer questions:** Ask 5 of the FAQs you added. The answers must match.
- **Contact and pricing questions:** Check that the phone number, email, and prices are accurate.
- **Product or service questions:** Check that the product links work.
- **Business hours:** Ask "Are you open on Sunday?". The answer must match your hours.
- **Form submissions:** Fill in a test form and check that the entry appears in **Forms**, under **Submissions**.
- **Lead capture:** Chat as a visitor with the pre-chat form turned on, and check that the entry appears in **Leads**.
- **Website widget visibility:** Open your live website in an incognito window. The widget must appear in the bottom corner, open and close correctly, work on mobile, and not be blocked by your website's security settings.

<Note>
  **If the live widget is missing:** Check that the script is in the head section and has the correct `data-key`, clear your cache, turn off your ad blocker, and click **Verify** again in Integration Center.

  **If answers are wrong:** Fix the Knowledge Base, re-crawl your pages, and test again.
</Note>

## Step 7: Monitor Results

After setup, check these areas regularly: every day during the first week, then every week.

- **Dashboard:** Leads, traffic, the 30-day activity feed, and credits left.
- **[Website Overview](/website-overview):** Audit scores. Click **Re-scan** after making changes, and download the PDF report to share with clients.
- **[SEO Intelligence](/seo-intelligence):** Domain, keywords, competitors, backlinks, and AI visibility. Click **Re-Crawl** after updating your content.
- **[Customer Inbox](/customer-inbox-and-leads):** Reply to chats, take over high-value chats, and check ratings.
- **[Billing](/profile-billing-and-team):** Your plan, credit top-ups (`100/500/1000`), transactions, and downgrade checks. When credits run low, scans stop.
- **Support Tickets:** Create a ticket if you are stuck, and track its status: OPEN, IN_PROGRESS, or RESOLVED. Check the Activity Log for changes made by your team.

<Tip>
  Turn on automatic jobs in **Crawlers & Jobs** for the website audit, security scan, Knowledge Base sync, and SEO research, so your reports stay fresh without manual clicks.
</Tip>
